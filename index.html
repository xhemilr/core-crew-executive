Questions

Clear answers, up front.

Ask on WhatsApp
Who handles the paperwork and work permit?

We do. Core Crew Solutions runs the whole process up to the work permit and completes all required documentation, in accordance with applicable procedures and regulations.

What types of workers can you supply?

Every level — from helpers and general labourers to skilled tradespeople, foremen, engineers and architects — across construction, electrical, hospitality, manufacturing, logistics, maintenance and more.

How many workers can I request?

Typically teams of 3, 5 or 10 workers, as well as individual skilled workers. Larger requirements are discussed individually.

Where do the workers come from?

Workers are mainly sourced internationally from India, Nepal and the Philippines.

Who provides accommodation?

Accommodation is provided by the company employing the workers.

How much does it cost?

Pricing is prepared individually according to worker category, number of workers, assignment duration and operational requirements.

How long does the process take?

It depends on the roles, the number of workers and permit processing. We give you a realistic timeline together with your quote.

Can I request a role that isn’t listed?

Yes. Tell us what you need — we source workers according to your operational requirements.
